# Native H2 exciton–vibration demonstration

Generated by `examples/bse/h2_ep_coupling.py`, with native GradSCF integrals,
RHF/3-21G, G0W0 and restricted singlet TDA-BSE. No external SCF or experimental
input is used. The full finite-basis TDA space has three states, including the
dark second state. Temperature is 300 K and numerical eta is 10 meV for all
three curves. A 2.5 meV-or-finer photon grid resolves those numerical poles.

The AD probe rescales the linear excitation vertices, holding mode energies,
quadratic operators, bare H_X and dipoles fixed. It is a derivative of this
specified model, not a full nuclear-coordinate derivative of the entire
SCF/GW/mode-generation workflow. Absolute AD/FD difference is 6.70e-11 Mb.

## Physical scope

The normal-mode energy and linear MO vertices use analytic native Hessian
contractions and implicit RHF response. Quadratic vertices use symmetric
reconverged displacements with maximum Cartesian motion 0.002 Bohr, in the
same maximum-overlap transported MO frame. G0W0 is frozen under displacement;
the electronic BSE kernel is frozen for this example. The one-body excitation
vertices therefore project screened HF electronic-minus-hole operators.

This is a one-phonon Fan/DW **stress example**. The largest excitation vertex
over the phonon quantum is 6.2054, so a weak-coupling or one-phonon expansion is
not a controlled quantitative description of H2's full vibrational spectrum.
The multiple poles shown are those of the stated finite effective model. No
experimental agreement, multiphonon Franck–Condon envelope, phonon feedback
or Herzberg–Teller dipole variation is claimed. A quantitative application
would require convergence of electronic/basis/phonon spaces and an adequate
strong-coupling treatment. The model assumptions and population/passivity
boundary are documented in `src/gradscf/bse/ep_coupling/README.md`.

## Artifacts

- `h2_bse_phonons.png` / `.pdf`: bare BSE, Fan, Fan+DW absorption and vertices.
  The first-feature zoom and full photon range include shifted/auxiliary poles.
- `absorption.csv`: photon energy in eV and three cross sections in Mb.
- `inputs.npz`: bare excitation/mode energies, projected linear/quadratic
  operators in Ha, dipoles in Bohr and nuclear coordinates in Bohr.
- `results.json`: unrounded values, JAX/backend/dtype and invocation time.
