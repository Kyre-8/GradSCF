# A07 — External-functional parameter sensitivities

A07 reuses the public external-functional and training examples. Current
`jy_gpu_1` runs passed for the three-mode H2 energy example, the H2 density-response
training example, and eight focused derivative tests. The frozen source and
executed-script hashes are archived; [commands and environment](../A03_A07_RUNTIME.md)
identify the current CPU float64 snapshot. This adds evidence and navigation,
not a new training engine or a spectroscopy dependency on neural training.

| Entry | Differentiated parameters and target | Density/reference treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [External functional](../../../examples/training/external_functional.py) | External Flax parameters; energy from a synthetic teacher | Fixed-density holds initial HF density; explicit/implicit relax the learned SCF with the same initial network |
| [DFPT response target](../../../examples/training/dfpt_response.py) | External density-matrix MLP parameters; AO `dD/dF` for `h(F)=h(0)+F z` | Explicit/implicit include the relaxed reference and response; geometry/basis fixed |
| [FCI-supervised history](../../../examples/training/h2_fci_results/README.md) | MLP parameters; one H2 FCI energy label | Three modes, 100 accepted updates each; FCI density is diagnostic only |
| [Force supervision](../../../examples/training/train_neural_scf_forces.py) | Neural parameters through molecular force loss | Separate native-integral/SCF mixed-derivative example, not rerun as part of this A07 group |

Both current molecular scripts use H2/6-31G*, seed 0, CPU float64 and three Adam
updates per mode. The energy example's grid-local network corrects Dirac
exchange; it is not a claim of a chemically accurate XC functional. The response
example's flattened AO-density network is tied to the fixed AO basis/order.
Its target has units of AO density per atomic electric-field unit; the label is
generated by a teacher network, not by an external chemical reference.

The two response runs each accepted all three updates and reported converged
response. The energy modes have different starting losses because fixed-density
and relaxed-SCF evaluation are different objectives. The evidence is in
[energy log](../../../artifacts/ad_showcase/provenance/A07_external_functional/A07_external_functional.log) and [response log](../../../artifacts/ad_showcase/provenance/A07_external_functional/A07_dfpt_response.log).
These short loss decreases demonstrate the API/derivative chain, not chemical
accuracy, transferability or a ranking of training modes.

The current [focused test log](../../../artifacts/ad_showcase/provenance/A07_external_functional/A07_focused.log) reports **8 passed, 17 deselected
in 198.43 s**, no skips. The reused tests check three-mode parameter gradients
against reconverged FD; explicit/implicit density-response parameter derivatives
include reference relaxation; a density-response forward result is compared
with two independently perturbed ground-state solves; and response-loss
gradients/accepted updates are checked. Their small two-AO analytic fixture
tests derivative semantics. It does not turn the molecular synthetic teacher
into a chemical oracle.

The longer FCI history and its [plot](../../../examples/training/h2_fci_results/loss_curves.png)
are deliberately kept as **2026-09-28 Apple M4 / JAX 0.8.1 historical evidence**.
The independently constructed PySCF FCI energy/density checks are within
`3.56e-15 Eh` and `8.89e-16`. However, after 100 steps the self-consistent model
energy error is still about `0.29041 Eh`, and its common post-training SCF loss
does not beat the fixed-density-trained model. Energy-only supervision at one
geometry does not establish density accuracy or generalization. The existing
CSVs/JSON/plots are linked without altering their data or old timing provenance.
The binary final parameter checkpoints are not included in that result bundle.
